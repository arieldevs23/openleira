# /brag plan — OpenLeira

## Planning rubric

1. **What is it?** OpenLeira, a self-hosted workspace for AI coding agents. You give work to an Orchestrator, which splits it across a team of agents (Frontend, Backend, QA…) on a canvas. The agents run in parallel without colliding, and QA checks the work before it counts as done.
2. **Who is it for?** Developers and small teams who already use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or OpenCode and want them working as one team on their own server.
3. **Strongest claim?** "One prompt, one AI team, on your own server."
4. **Most visual moment?** The team graph: work moves from the Orchestrator into Frontend and Backend in parallel, then into QA, and the QA ring turns into a check mark.
5. **Name and identity:** OPENLEIRA, gothic dark. bg `#0A0A0B`, surface `#141416`, text `#F2F2F0`, accent `#C9CCD4`, ok `#7FB08A`. Cinzel, Playfair Display and Inter. The black rose from the landing page.
6. **What's absurd or fun?** A solo developer suddenly has a whole team. Treat it like a blockbuster.
7. **Tone:** `cinematic`, with the freeform direction "a semi-mysterious launch trailer driven by text, Indonesian copy that is cool and simple, a morphing background behind the rose, a small clear simulated graph (not a screenshot)".
8. **Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 23.5 s.
9. **Audio:** bundled track `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12` (≈110 BPM). It has a quiet build until about 11 s and energy bumps at 11.5 s and 17.5 s. Sparse SFX: a click when the message is sent, a soft impact on reveals, a bell under the logo.

## Angle

Your AI finally has a team. The story is carried almost entirely by text: short lines that pop, stretch in, and slide out of a mask. Behind them is one continuous gothic tracery field that keeps morphing (circle → rose window → quatrefoil → star) and never cuts. The product shows up as two small, clear simulations: a work chat and a four-node team graph.
