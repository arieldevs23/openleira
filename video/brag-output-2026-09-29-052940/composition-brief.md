# Hyperframes Composition Brief: OpenLeira

## Objective
Create a short, semi-mysterious launch trailer for OpenLeira, driven by text.

## Output
- Composition directory: `composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080, 30 fps
- Duration: 23.5 seconds

## Source Material
- Project root: `/home/user/openleira` (the app, `landing/`, `docs/DESIGN.md`, README)
- Product name: OpenLeira
- Tagline / strongest claim: "Satu prompt. Satu tim AI. Di server lo sendiri."
- Key UI to recreate: the work chat ("ke Orchestrator") and the team graph (Orchestrator → Frontend, Backend → QA), both simplified, large and readable
- Copy that must appear verbatim: "Satu prompt.", "Satu tim AI.", "Di server lo sendiri.", "Lo kasih kerjaan.", "OPENLEIRA", "tim AI lo sendiri.", "openleira.online"

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: a mysterious trailer where text does most of the work, with few visuals, seamless transitions, and a morphing background behind the rose
- Interpretation: short declarative lines with generous holds. Every exit is a dissolve (blur, slight scale, fade), never a hard cut. A single tracery background runs the whole film.
- Hook: "Satu prompt." popping letter by letter on the first beat
- Outro: the rose, then "OPENLEIRA", then "tim AI lo sendiri."
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, screenshots, equalizer or particle visuals

## Visual Identity
- Background `#0A0A0B`, surface `#141416`, text `#F2F2F0`, muted `#8A8A90`, accent `#C9CCD4`, ok `#7FB08A`
- Display: Cinzel 600/700. Titles: Playfair Display 500/700. UI: Inter 400–600 (local woff2 files).
- Visual reference: the landing page's black rose with a radial mask

## Storyboard
See `brag-plan.md`. The scenes are Hook (3.8 s), Rose (3.8 s), Give it work (3.8 s), Team (5.5 s) and Finale (6.6 s).

## Audio
- Audio role: cinematic support
- Music: `assets/music/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at 0.36, with a fade-out at the end
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset. Locks at 8.74, 17.47 and 22.93 s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Bass drives the morph field's glow and scale.
- SFX: click on send, soft impacts on the hook, the send and the QA check, and a heavy bell under the logo
