# OpenLeira — brag plan

**What it is:** a self-hosted workspace where a whole AI team (orchestrator, planner, designers, engineers, QA) works on your app folder, driven from one canvas.
**For:** developers and indie builders who run coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode) and want them working together on their own server.
**What sets it apart:** you hand out work as a prompt or a list, the orchestrator splits it across teams by the flow you drew, one team does one thing at a time, and nothing counts as done until the audit agent passes it.
**Most impressive claim:** one prompt → a planned, parallel, audited team run, on your own machine.
**Visual hook:** the real Node Design canvas lighting up team by team on the beat.
**Real UI shown:** the app's own `OfficeCanvas` and `CoordinatorDock` components, rendered with the app's built stylesheet and fonts (not rebuilt).
**Tone:** cinematic — dark gothic identity (docs/DESIGN.md tokens, the black rose), big serif type, the song's energy drives the cuts.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

**Music:** the user's track (≈103 BPM), 0–24.4 s: the hit at 0.5 s opens the hook, the break at 4–5 s carries the brand reveal, the second dip at 22.5 s carries the outro; faded out by 24.4 s.
